# List, claim, correct or remove a listing

Every listing on NJ Home Sale Checklist comes from the NJ DCA List of Certified Lead Evaluation Contractors. Use this form to claim your free listing (add a phone number, website or service area), fix an error, ask to be added, or ask for a listing to be removed.

## What happens next

1. We check the request against the NJ DCA record. For claims and corrections we confirm by replying to an email address or phone number on that record, or one you can show is yours.
2. Approved changes go live with the next site update, usually within 5 business days.
3. **Removal:** we remove personal details (for example a home address or personal phone) on request. If you are no longer licensed or certified, tell us and we'll drop the listing. Because listings mirror a public record, we may keep the business name and license status while it stays on the official list.

Listings are free and ordered by location and name, never by payment.
