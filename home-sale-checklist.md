## Frequently asked questions

**What certificates does a NJ seller need?**

Statewide: the smoke/CO alarm certificate for 1-2 family homes, and well test results if the home is on a private well. Many towns add a certificate of occupancy or continued-occupancy inspection, which this site notes where verified.

**Is a radon or oil tank inspection required?**

Not by state law. Both are common buyer inspections.
