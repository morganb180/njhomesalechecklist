# Smoke and CO alarm certificate in Clifton, NJ

Selling or renting out a one- or two-family home in Clifton (Passaic County)? You need a certificate of smoke alarm and carbon monoxide alarm compliance before the new occupant moves in.

## Clifton: who issues it official source

**Issuing agency:** Clifton Fire Department.  
**Official source:** <https://cliftonnj.org/DocumentCenter/View/127/Dwelling-Application-Packet-PDF> (found 2026-10-12).

City dwelling application packet. Search summaries report $125 per unit for resale, with expedited fees of $200 or $300.

Fees, lead times and forms change. Confirm with the town before you schedule. Last checked 2026-10-12.

## Statewide rules

* **When:** before a one- or two-family home or attached single-family home is sold, leased or otherwise changes occupants ([N.J.A.C. 5:70-2.3](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/new-jersey/N-J-A-C-5-70-2-3)).
* **Who issues it:** the local enforcing agency (usually the town's fire prevention bureau, fire official or fire district). Where no local agency enforces the Uniform Fire Code, the NJ DCA Division of Fire Safety issues it, through [its online portal](https://firesolutions.dca.nj.gov/ultra-fire-home/smoke-application-create/). The agency may accept a certification *in lieu of inspection* on its own form ([DCA in-lieu form](https://www.nj.gov/dca/divisions/dfs/forms/bfce/certificationinlieu.pdf)).
* **Validity:** not transferable. If the change of occupant doesn't happen within 6 months, a new application is required. Some towns set shorter windows: Woodbridge 60 days and Cherry Hill 90 days, per town documents.
* **State default fee** ([N.J.A.C. 5:70-2.9(d)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/new-jersey/N-J-A-C-5-70-2-9)): $45 if requested more than 10 business days before the change of occupant, $90 at 4-10 business days, $161 at fewer than 4. Towns with a local enforcing agency may set their own fees by ordinance, and many do.
* **Fire extinguisher:** the certificate historically also covered a portable kitchen extinguisher (hence the names CSACMAPFEC and, older, CSDCMFEC). Several municipal pages (e.g. Toms River, Sayreville) report that **as of Feb 3, 2025 a portable extinguisher is no longer required** for this certificate. confirm with town: we haven't confirmed the statewide rule text. Check your town's current checklist.
