About NJ Home Sale Checklist

NJ Home Sale Checklist is a free guide to the inspections and certificates involved in selling or buying a New Jersey home: the smoke/CO alarm certificate, well testing, radon, oil tanks and lead, with a directory of DCA-certified lead evaluation contractors.

It is for New Jersey sellers, buyers, agents and closing attorneys. Listings: NJ DCA certified lead evaluation contractors (list revised 8/11/2026). NJDEP lists (well-testing labs, radon businesses, UST closure firms) could not be fetched automatically. Those pages link to the official lookup instead.

How it's built

  • Listings are rebuilt from the official source files on each refresh; every listing shows its source and retrieval date.
  • Rules and cost guides link the statute, regulation, agency page or official fee schedule they rely on. Where we couldn't confirm something from an official source, we say so or leave it out.
  • Nothing here is legal advice. Requirements change, so confirm with the agency or town before you act.

Who operates NJ Home Sale Checklist

NJ Home Sale Checklist (njhomesalechecklist.com) is operated by NJ Home Sale Checklist. Contact us through the contact page.

Independence

NJ Home Sale Checklist is independent. It isn't affiliated with any government agency, licensing board or contractor, and a listing is not an endorsement. See how we make money, how we verify listings and about our data.
