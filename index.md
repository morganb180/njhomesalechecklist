# Find a certified lead evaluator you can actually check.

NJ Home Sale Checklist is a free guide to the inspections and certificates in a New Jersey home sale, and a directory of DCA-certified lead evaluation contractors. Every listing shows the certification number from the NJ DCA list (revised 8/11/2026, retrieved 2026-10-12).

181 DCA-certified lead evaluation contractors and 25 town smoke/CO certificate guides (11 with official sources). Hunterdon and Salem have no contractor address on the list. For certified well labs, radon businesses and oil-tank firms, each guide links NJDEP's official lookup.

Search by town or county to open that county's contractor list. A quote request connects you with up to 3 nearby DCA-certified contractors. They contact you directly. Phone, email and website are not published. County lists are ordered by location and name.

## What a NJ home sale needs

| Item | Required? |
| --- | --- |
| [Smoke & CO alarm certificate](https://njhomesalechecklist.com/smoke-co-certificate/) | Yes, 1-2 family homes, before the new occupant moves in |
| [Private well test (PWTA)](https://njhomesalechecklist.com/well-testing/) | Yes, if the home is on a private well |
| Town certificate of occupancy | Depends on the town |
| [Radon test](https://njhomesalechecklist.com/radon/) | Not required; common buyer inspection |
| [Oil tank sweep](https://njhomesalechecklist.com/oil-tanks/) | Not required; closures need a certified firm |
| [Lead](https://njhomesalechecklist.com/lead/) | Pre-1978 sales: federal disclosure and 10-day test window. Rentals: lead-safe certification |
