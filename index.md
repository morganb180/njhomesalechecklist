# New Jersey home sale checklist and certified pros

181 DCA-certified lead evaluation contractors and 25 town smoke/CO certificate guides (11 with official sources). For certified well labs, radon businesses and oil-tank firms, each guide links NJDEP's official lookup.

Selling or buying in NJ? Start with the [checklist](https://njhomesalechecklist.com/home-sale-checklist/).

## What a NJ home sale needs

| Item | Required? |
| --- | --- |
| [Smoke & CO alarm certificate](https://njhomesalechecklist.com/smoke-co-certificate/) | Yes, 1-2 family homes, before the new occupant moves in |
| [Private well test (PWTA)](https://njhomesalechecklist.com/well-testing/) | Yes, if the home is on a private well |
| Town certificate of occupancy | Depends on the town |
| [Radon test](https://njhomesalechecklist.com/radon/) | Not required; common buyer inspection |
| [Oil tank sweep](https://njhomesalechecklist.com/oil-tanks/) | Not required; closures need a certified firm |
| [Lead](https://njhomesalechecklist.com/lead/) | Pre-1978 sales: federal disclosure and 10-day test window. Rentals: lead-safe certification |
