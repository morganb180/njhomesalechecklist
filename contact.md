# Contact NJ Home Sale Checklist

NJ Home Sale Checklist is a small independent directory and guide. We read every message. We aren't a contractor, inspector or government agency, and we can't do home-sale certificates and lead evaluations work ourselves.

## Email

[hello@njhomesalechecklist.com](mailto:hello@njhomesalechecklist.com): questions, data corrections and advertising. When writing about a specific listing, please include its listing ID (for example the contractor's DCA certification number).

## Listings

To claim, correct, add or remove a listing, use the [listing request form](https://njhomesalechecklist.com/list-your-business/).
